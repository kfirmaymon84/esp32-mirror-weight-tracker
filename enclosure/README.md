# Enclosure — 4" Smart Mirror (E32R40T)

3D-printable case for the LCDWiki **E32R40T** board (ESP32-32E + 4.0" ST7796S
480×320 + resistive touch), with room for a **16×9×4 mm speaker** and a **GY-530
(VL53L0X) ToF** module. Powered over USB-C (no battery). Hangs on the wall *or*
stands on a shelf with the included stand.

Outer size: **123.2 × 93.8 × 24.3 mm**.

| File (in `out/`) | What |
|---|---|
| `front_shell.stl` / `.step` | Frame + walls. Display window, ToF window (top), speaker grille (bottom), USB-C opening (right side), 4 PCB posts, 4 corner pillars |
| `back_cover.stl` / `.step` | Back plate — **snaps in, no screws** (4 hooks + 2 locating tabs). 2 wall keyholes with closed hoods, BOOT/RESET pin holes, pry notch |
| `stand.stl` / `.step` | Desk stand. Two pegs drop into the keyholes; device leans back 15° |
| `preview_*.png` | Rendered previews |

The model is generated by `build_case.py` (CadQuery). It also loads the real board
model (`E32R40T_3D.step`) and checks that no case part touches any component — the
run prints `INTERFERENCE CHECK: PASS`. Change a number at the top of the script and
re-run to regenerate everything:

```
py -3 build_case.py      # parts + interference check
py -3 render.py          # preview PNGs
```

## Printing

| Part | Orientation | Supports |
|---|---|---|
| Front shell | **front face down** on the bed | none (window/ToF/USB openings bridge cleanly) |
| Back cover | **outside face down** | none (keyhole hood roofs bridge 9 mm) |
| Stand | **base down** | only under the two pegs |

PETG recommended (bathroom warmth/humidity); PLA is fine for a dry room.
0.2 mm layers, 3–4 walls, 20 % infill.

## Hardware

| Qty | Part | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | M3 × 6 pan-head (self-tapping into plastic, or with M3 heat-set inserts) | board → front-shell posts |
| — | hot glue | holds ToF module and speaker in their pockets |
| 2 | wall screws, head Ø < 8.5 mm, shank Ø < 4.2 mm, **70 mm apart, level** | wall mounting |

## Assembly

1. **ToF:** press the GY-530 into the pocket above the display window, **sensor facing
   the front**, and tack it with hot glue. Solder 4 wires (VIN, GND, SCL, SDA) and plug
   them into the board's **I2C** socket.
2. **Speaker:** press it into the pocket behind the grille under the window, membrane
   facing the front, glue lightly. Its lead runs behind the board to the **SPEAKER**
   socket on the top edge.
3. **Board:** lay it face-down into the front shell (USB-C toward the side opening) so
   the 4 holes sit on the 4 posts, and fit the 4 × M3×6 screws. The posts hold the glass
   **0.4 mm clear of the bezel**, so the touch layer is never pressed.
4. **Close:** line the cover up (the side tabs centre it) and press it in until all four
   hooks click. No screws.

**Opening:** put a coin or fingernail in the pry notch at the bottom seam and lift; the
hooks' catch faces are sloped so they release with a firm pull.

BOOT / RESET can be pressed through the two pin holes next to the USB-C end with a
paper clip.

**Snap-fit notes:** the hooks print standing up (the weaker direction for FDM), so they
are long and thin and only flex 0.55 mm (~1.5 % strain). Print in **PETG** for the most
forgiving hooks. If the fit is too tight or too loose on your printer, change
`SNAP_BARB` (catch height) or `SNAP_CLR` (gap to the wall) at the top of `build_case.py`.

## Mounting

* **Wall:** two keyholes, **70 mm apart**. Screw two screws into the wall, level, leaving
  ~3 mm of the head out; hook the device on and slide it down.
* **Shelf:** push the stand's pegs through the big ends of the keyholes and slide the
  device down until it sits on the stand's lip.

## Known unknowns — check on first print

* **ToF window position:** the GY-530's sensor location varies slightly between makers;
  the window (10 × 6 mm) is centred on the module. If the sensor sits off-centre on yours,
  widen `aper` in `build_case.py`.
* **USB-C plug:** the opening fits a typical 12 × 6.5 mm overmold. Very chunky plugs may
  need it widened.
